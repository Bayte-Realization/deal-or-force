# Roman restoration flags

Original game artwork generated with OpenAI image generation, then mechanically
resampled to the game's 384 × 256 flag format. No outside reference image was used.
These are fictional restoration designs, not claims about historical standards.

The generation brief was a flat, full-bleed 3:2 strategy-game flag: no pole, folds,
photographic background or surrounding frame; readable heraldry at small sizes.

| Asset | Design brief |
|---|---|
| `game/gfx/flags/flag_roman_empire_v2.png` | Crimson field, gold Roman eagle and SPQR inscription. |
| `game/gfx/flags/flag_eastern_roman_empire_v2.png` | Deep purple field, gold double-headed eagle and cross; fictional eastern restoration. |
| `game/gfx/flags/flag_western_roman_empire.png` | Oxblood field, gold single eagle, wreath and star, horizontal gold border stripes, no letters. |

Bindings live in `game/gfx/flags/94_roman_successors.json`; formation rules
live in `game/common/formable_nations/32_roman_successors.json`.
