# Constitutional movements in the 2024 sandbox

Four unnamed coalitions now use real organisations: Scottish National Party,
Plaid Cymru, Parti Québécois and Bavaria Party (Bayernpartei). Their historical
presence and constitutional objectives are supported by primary sources:

- [SNP constitutional position](https://www.snp.org/our-vision/constitution/).
- [Plaid Cymru constitution, revised November 2023](https://www.partyof.wales/constitution).
- [Plaid Cymru and SNP joint statement, 16 October 2023](https://www.partyof.wales/prosiect_gwleidyddol_ar_y_cyd_plaid_cymru_snp_shared_political_project).
- [Parti Québécois principles, adopted November 2019](https://cartedemembre.pq.org/static/pdfs/d%C3%A9claration.pdf).
- [Bayernpartei 2023 election programme](https://bayernpartei.de/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/Wahlprogramm-der-Bayernpartei-zur-Landtagswahl-2023.pdf).

The game does not equate these organisations with all residents or all members
of a culture. Support, finances, cadres and influence ranges are balancing
parameters, not polling percentages or verified party accounts. The small
Bavarian party therefore does not inherit the previous fictional coalition's
large starting following. Constitutional campaigning retains `nonviolence`
and `civic_independence`: it cannot spontaneously raise an armed insurgency.

Existing movement IDs and tags are preserved to keep mod scripts, flag
fallbacks and save references valid. The overlay is
`game/common/movements/40_real_constitutional_parties.json`; translations live
in the corresponding `247_real_constitutional_parties.json` files. Mods may
override any field with a later JSON or YAML document.

Existing regional flags identify the territory, not a licensed party logo.
Headquarters remain assigned to the coarse existing map regions; this change
does not assert that each marker is the party's exact street address.

Other fictional regional coalitions are still present and are explicitly
marked as sandbox abstractions. Replacing those requires separate historical
and organisational research; this change does not pretend that all movements
are already factual.
