# Deal or Force — EntertAlpha 0.6.3.1

11 November 2026 · pocket surrender and combat resolution fix

A focused fix on top of 0.6.3.0. Previous release content is retained.

## Release number 0.6.3.1

- The number **0.6.3.1** is the same in the main menu, new save metadata, the game, the
  rules library, the launcher and the Windows EXE, the Godot and export settings, the
  README files, the build instructions and this changelog.
- The save format, map version and network protocol are unchanged.

## Pocket surrender at zero morale

- Encircled defenders with no legal retreat now surrender when organisation is effectively
  exhausted — 0.5% or less. Supply or remaining stores no longer let them defend forever.
- Standard warfare accounting records prisoners and losses; once the defenders are gone,
  the winner captures the province and the battle ends. Troops can still retreat where
  a legal route exists; ordinary last-stand conditions remain in effect.
